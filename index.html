01

ALGEBRA

Solve: x + 9 = 14

Show answer and method

Subtract 9 from both sides: x = 14 − 9, so x = 5.

02

PERCENTAGES

Find 25% of 80.

Show answer and method

25% is one quarter. Calculate 80 ÷ 4, so the answer is 20.

03

GEOMETRY

Two angles are 35° and 85°. Find the third angle of a triangle.

Show answer and method

Angles in a triangle total 180°: 180° − 35° − 85° = 60°.
