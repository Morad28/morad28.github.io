About me

Welcome to my website. I am a mathematics teacher who believes that learning maths should be thoughtful, structured, and accessible. My aim is to help students understand not only how to solve a problem, but why a method works.

In my teaching, I focus on clear explanations, carefully chosen examples, and regular practice. I encourage students to ask questions, explain their reasoning, and see mistakes as a useful part of learning.

This website brings together information about my approach to teaching and a collection of learning materials for students. I hope it helps you study with greater confidence and independence.
