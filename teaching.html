LESSON NOTES

Start with a topic

01 / ALGEBRA

Solving equations

An equation stays balanced when you do the same operation to both sides.

Worked example2x + 5 = 17

Subtract 5: 2x = 12
Divide by 2: x = 6

02 / NUMBER

Percentages

A percentage describes a number of parts out of one hundred.

Worked example20% of 60

Write 20% as 0.20.
0.20 × 60 = 12

03 / GEOMETRY

Angles in a triangle

The interior angles of any triangle add up to 180°.

Worked example50° + 60° + x

x = 180° − 110°
Therefore, x = 70°

INDEPENDENT PRACTICE

Try these questions

Work them out on paper first, then reveal the solution to check your method.

1

Solve: x + 9 = 14

Reveal solution

Subtract 9 from both sides: x = 14 − 9, so x = 5.

2

Find 25% of 80.

Reveal solution

25% is one quarter. Calculate 80 ÷ 4, so the answer is 20.

3

Two angles in a triangle are 35° and 85°. Find the third angle.

Reveal solution

180° − 35° − 85° = 60°.
